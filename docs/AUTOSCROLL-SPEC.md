# Optional finish-and-swipe convenience

**Status:** AS-044/AS-059 experimental Android implementation; source-specific completion/action qualification remains open. Updated 2026-11-08.
**Responsibility:** Owns optional session controls, playback-loop decisions and bounded scrolling actions.
**Related specifications:** [Decisions](DECISIONS.md), [Social](SOCIAL-MEDIA-SPEC.md), [Permissions](PERMISSIONS.md), [Contracts](NATIVE-CONTRACTS.md), [Human factors](HUMAN-FACTORS.md), [Testing](TESTING.md).
**Unresolved feasibility:** Actual video-end signals differ by app/version; F-08 Play accessibility review remains open. A layout encounter is not playback or completion.

Current beta override (ADR-075/AS-059, 2026-11-04): retain the user's session On/Off choice through manual scrolling and unsupported/media transitions while suspending gestures and invalidating queued tokens. One control only: `Autoscroll` with a drawn play icon appears expanded while Off; the same button with a drawn pause icon appears just below counts while On, including minimized. Header taps/dragging never toggle it. Established Home/feed states retain committed-count display between posts; unknown/private/input/foreign/locked contexts remain excluded. First use still needs the separate convenience disclosure. Session termination clears ephemeral state. Direct fresh viewer/source/window/item/consent checks precede every gesture; On while waiting does not mean a gesture is authorized. Historical automatic-Stop and dual-control descriptions below are superseded for this candidate.

## Choice and scope

Current media boundary (2026-11-07): the five-second still-item convenience applies only to a positively classified `mediaKind=photo` item without playback progress, including a qualified image ad. Missing video/unknown progress is unavailable, never evidence of a photo or completion. Starting or resuming on such an explicit photo may establish its dwell directly without first waiting for a numeric video transition. Source/window/page/epoch/freshness/visibility fences still apply; stop/interruption clears the dwell. A fresh final scan must confirm the same photo/no-progress classification before its gesture; a numeric video proposal likewise requires valid current numeric progress. This repairs the earlier ambiguous-null fallback without adding a timer substitute for video end. The choice remains ephemeral, separately disclosed and experimental.

The user prioritizes optional auto-scroll within the four-app counter MVP. It remains independent of counting and never enables observation or the counter. The initial implementation requires those separate choices so a qualified viewer and an always-visible Stop control are available. Convenience settings explain the action and numeric progress inspection before affirmative Allow. This permission is memory-only for the current service lifetime, defaults off, and never resumes an active run on restart. No Room migration or durable action history is introduced.

Allow is followed by a separate Start inside the viewer. Stop is visible even while the counter is collapsed. Turning off the option, source pause/revocation, hiding the HUD, service interruption/destruction, device lock, app/window/epoch change, unknown or excluded context, missing/ambiguous progress, rejected/cancelled gesture or unconfirmed next page stops future actions. A dispatched 300ms swipe can finish after Stop; there is no queued series of gestures. User scrolling outside the in-flight transition stops the run; clicks stop immediately, including during confirmation. A maximum 750ms pager-scroll tail after confirmed arrival is treated as part of our own swipe settling. A different page, window or epoch still stops the run during that tail, and Stop is never delayed. An unknown viewer transition is tolerated only for the bounded confirmation of our own swipe; other contexts stop immediately.

## Playback evidence and actions

Only separately admitted installed-version profiles can consume numeric progress. Current Instagram/Facebook/TikTok candidates use a single visible bottom-edge full-width SeekBar with finite values. Instagram requires scrubber; TikTok 47.5.3 requires vt6 with a 0–10000 range. Missing, hidden or duplicate player ranges remain unavailable. Structural acquisition reads ranges only after the separate choice, excludes editable/password/foreign/invisible nodes, bounds traversal to 192 nodes (384 for ADR-063 TikTok) and eight ranges, and withholds truncated/ambiguous evidence. Numeric progress is not saved to history, diagnostics or the bridge during normal use. Operator-only development investigation may inspect bounded numeric progress, static IDs/classes, fixed interface tokens and exact numeric time formats locally; unmatched text is discarded and no raw content is retained.

ADR-080 supports looping and non-looping players. Require the same qualified app/window/page/epoch, at least eight small monotonic advances spanning two seconds, and fresh samples. Observed playback may begin midway. Completion is inferred from either a near-end (at least 98%) to beginning (at most 5%) loop, or two consecutive numeric terminal samples at or above 0.999 after those observed advances. The latter accommodates Facebook's measured 999/1000 terminal step without waiting forever for replay. Frozen/end-only initial values, partial-progress pauses, large seek jumps, changed contexts and stale samples cannot establish completion. Elapsed time alone never triggers a video gesture. This remains numeric completion inference, not a durable exact completion metric; manual seek, buffering and source-specific end behavior need qualification.

Immediately before a single upward swipe, revalidate current root package/window, source consent, OS access, active epoch, interactive/unlocked state, visible counter and a fresh bounded viewer scan/page. Use the center column, avoiding side actions; never like, comment, follow, share or tap a provider control automatically. Require a confirmed different page within 2.5 seconds before continuing. A qualified new page without playback progress confirms arrival but suspends actions while preserving the On choice; arrival and readiness for another swipe are separate facts. TikTok pre-action revalidation repeats its separately consented transient page matcher rather than dropping that identity during the final scan. A main-thread deadline stops the run even if no further scan arrives; a late page cannot confirm it. Stale, nonfinite or out-of-range progress cannot enable Start or maintain a run. Completion proposals never increment counts; ordinary observed settled-page commits remain the only counter authority.

YouTube stays unavailable without a usable numeric playback signal. TikTok is an experimental exact-version candidate; real loop/action and negative-case acceptance remain required. Do not substitute a fixed duration, count detector, caption change or arbitrary screen timer for video end. Current Google Play guidance permits deterministic human-defined automation subject to its other accessibility/disclosure requirements; it does not preapprove this app. [Play AccessibilityService policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10964491?hl=en), [Android gesture API](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/accessibilityservice/AccessibilityService#dispatchGesture(android.accessibilityservice.GestureDescription,%20android.accessibilityservice.AccessibilityService.GestureResultCallback,%20android.os.Handler)) (reviewed 2026-10-29).

## Acceptance

Synthetic tests cover one-shot loop decisions, near-end-only/paused/frozen/seek/stale/changed-page/window/epoch and invalid numeric input, plus ambiguous/unrelated slider rejection. Own-window controls must verify separate Allow/Start/Stop and a visible collapsed-counter Stop. Runtime fixtures additionally exercise one-shot delivery, token cancellation, timely and late next-page confirmation, missing-callback deadlines and fresh evidence after re-enabling. Live app trials must cover real end/loop, next page confirmation, pause, buffering, manual seek/scroll, photo/ad/live/unknown surfaces, comments, switch, lock, revocation and service restart. Tests bind actual artifacts and installed versions. Unrun cases remain open; synthetic tests never certify a target app or Play approval.

The older `tools/reels_phone.py qualify/autoscroll` modes refer to the pre-ADR-066 retired harness; their historical 118 assertions are not current-product acceptance. For current synthetic session regression, build preview/test with `tools/android_build.py`, verify artifact and test-package ownership, then run `adb -s <selected-phone> shell am instrument --no-restart -w -r -e autoScrollSafety true com.attentionstats.debug.test/com.attentionstats.qualification.AnalyticsInstrumentation`. This invokes the product state machine on physical ART with synthetic frames and counts callback proposals without dispatching input. It refuses an active convenience run and restores the prior enabled/disclosure choice. Require explicit PASS and instrumentation code -1; remove only the test installed for the trial and restore original accessibility settings. Video-loop, missing-progress, first-photo, photo reclassification, interrupted dwell and Stop cases do not establish real source completion/gesture acceptance. Live operator trials remain separate under the already-disclosed testing authorization.

## Non-interruptive numeric investigation

ADR-053 keeps detection out of the target UI: no pause tap, control reveal or seek is performed to obtain a production signal. The operator-only numeric-control probe can traverse up to 192 selected-package nodes, including hidden nodes, and emits only ranges from the known player IDs. Hidden values do not become production progress. A separate shell-authorized media-session probe reads duration and numeric playback state only for the selected foreground source, then drops its temporary shell identity. No notification access, media-control action, screenshot capability or production metadata adapter is added.

The bounded physical test found YouTube Shorts media duration/position/state all zero and no active TikTok media session. A media-session record alone is insufficient: duration, playing position and item transitions must correspond to the actual viewer. TikTok additionally exposed changing visible vt6 range values after a controlled transition, enabling the numeric candidate above. These observations are version/route scoped. Android documents [media-session access](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/session/MediaSessionManager#getActiveSessions(android.content.ComponentName)) and [duration metadata](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/MediaMetadata#METADATA_KEY_DURATION); neither guarantees that a provider publishes useful values.


## Current phone qualification modes

`playbackProbe=<source>` performs bounded, no-action source sampling and reports only numeric-availability/context aggregates. A completed probe with zero eligible viewer samples is not source acceptance. It refuses an active convenience run; source grant or foreground loss stops it. Detailed diagnostic material stays local under the maintainer's no-upload instruction.

`autoScrollLive=true -e autoScrollApp <source>` requires an already-consented supported viewer, a connected service, visible HUD support and no conflicting source block/slot. It preserves the current convenience choice and reminder settings. It temporarily disables reminders, uses at most three operator setup swipes to find an early numeric-video candidate, then allows at most one automatic attempt/confirmed transition before Stop. The current test expands the actual HUD as needed, clicks its Off control to Start and its On control to Stop after confirmation; direct session calls no longer stand in for those user-facing actions. It restores a test-expanded panel when still available and restores reminder/convenience choices even if that UI cleanup fails. Service connection, source consent and HUD readiness fail separately before setup gestures. Independent settled-item evidence requires changed identity plus restarted numeric playback, explicit photo classification, or strongly distinct metadata keys for an otherwise qualified no-progress viewer. Missing next-item progress is separately reported and never called repeated end support. The trial is bounded to 150 seconds after Start: a long video which has not reached its numeric end supplies no positive end evidence. Ordinary consented observations remain history.

The test-only `disableBackup=true` operator mode turns off automatic Android backup through its existing owner without requesting a transfer. It is used only under the maintainer's explicit no-upload testing instruction; the saved opt-out is deliberately retained. No public production control, account or network recipient is added.


The final waiting-state UI retains the same single Autoscroll action. When On without usable progress it shows a noninteractive waiting hint; counting pause also retains the Stop control. Source-specific end readiness is independent of that control's availability. The maintainer-requested session checkpoint and source matrix are in [State](STATE.md#new-session-handoff-auto-scroll-investigation-2026-11-07); none of the unqualified sources is marked working from the presence of a button.
