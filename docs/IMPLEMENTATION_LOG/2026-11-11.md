### AS-058 repair

The initial pending-file check did not cover the interval spent waiting for coordinator snapshot export. A restore committed in that interval could coexist with a later `fullBackupFile` offer. `withBackupAdmission` now checks all pending siblings under the existing pending-file process lock and retains that lock through request admission or the final staged-file creation/handoff. Export and its 25-second coordinator wait remain outside the lock. Pending delivery/read/discard share this serialization. If delivery wins during export, the final offer is rejected; if handoff wins, delivery commits after it returns. This is a process-local ordering guarantee, not evidence of actual simultaneous framework callbacks.

The new `backupAdmission=true` test uses unique test-owned no-backup directories and synthetic bytes. Its 19 physical assertions reproduce separated-check admission, force delivery during simulated export, check both lock-order outcomes with latches/blocked worker threads, reject recovery/staging siblings and verify exception release.
