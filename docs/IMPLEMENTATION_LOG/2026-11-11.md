## Changes

Continued on clean `develop` from `217ddfeac74f8222a0faf4816f7e9d694c1a7089`, without delegation. AS-060's dismissal previously used the bounded observation queue's `trySend`: rejection could leave the service's stale-reminder fence set indefinitely. Dismissal now suspends asynchronously until that same serialized queue has capacity, resets the in-memory reminder, then posts acknowledgement. The UI thread remains free and the cover stays removed while pending. Duplicate pending service requests are ignored; a late acknowledgement cannot render through a destroyed service. No database, source consent, threshold or observation admission change.

AS-058 adds an isolated source-key-loss recovery test. It exports a synthetic profile-3 archive, closes the source store, deletes only its task-owned Keystore alias and verifies failed source reopening preserves the database/wrapper hashes without regenerating the alias. It stages and replaces into a separately keyed destination, closes/reopens it, and checks the original fact ID/origin, video total, distinct destination installation epoch and disabled source configurations. A corrupt envelope is rejected before replacement and destination totals/origin remain intact. All synthetic stores, aliases and archive buffers are cleaned up. No framework transport or real history replacement is invoked.
