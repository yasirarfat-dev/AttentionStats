## HUD and encounter clarification — ADR-052 / 2026-10-29

User explicitly distinguishes temporary HUD hiding, persistent display settings and pausing collection. HUD Hide lasts until departure from the current app while observations continue; only app display settings persistently turn the counter off. Pause counting remains a separate action until resumed. The user also requests compact HUD access to auto-scroll, with no first-use explanations in the HUD. If consent is needed, open the app's Convenience screen; accepted session toggles then work directly. No change grants observation, starts automation or treats hidden UI as disabled collection.

Video counts across qualified routes mean encounters, including autoplay-off items, rather than only played videos. Dedicated-viewer, inline Home and other surface lanes require their own qualification. Keep encountered, playback-observed and finished definitions distinct; no failproof or unique-content claim.

## Non-interruptive auto-scroll signals — ADR-053 / 2026-10-29

The user prioritizes counting and auto-scroll across the four-source MVP and rejects UI interruption while obtaining progress: do not pause, reveal provider controls or change the viewing interface to trigger scrolling. Prefer available numeric progress and investigate published duration/position metadata. Keep the existing independent Allow/Start/Stop flow. Image processing was initially deferred because of its computational cost. ADR-054 below subsequently authorizes a measured progress-bar-only prototype; no screenshot capability or image-reading implementation has yet been added. Missing numeric signals remain unavailable rather than becoming a timer. Narrow source candidates and actual live results must remain distinguishable. Broader onboarding and product expansion wait behind this MVP work.

## Home counting before dedicated-viewer automation — ADR-054 / 2026-10-29

The user prioritizes Instagram/Facebook Home-route reel detection before further auto-scroll expansion. Observe authorized visible comparison behavior privately; do not publish comparison-app names, assets, traces or personal totals. Qualify Home-opened and inline media independently, preserving encounter semantics and source consent. Auto-scroll is limited to dedicated reel viewers. Its inactive Start/options belong inside the HUD menu opened by tapping the counter; an active Stop remains immediately accessible. The HUD offers session-only hiding, while persistent display-off remains in app settings. A local progress-bar-pixel prototype is authorized for missing numeric signals, with measured capture/CPU costs, no OCR/image retention/upload and no interruption or provider-UI manipulation. Existing source/control disclosures must accurately describe any future implemented capture before it runs. No capture implementation or Home-count support is claimed by this decision.
